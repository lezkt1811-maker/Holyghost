    FAQ

    Common questions

    What should I do if a pipe bursts?

    Shut off the main water valve right away (usually in the basement near the front wall, or by the water heater), turn off the water heater, then call us at 816-879-9346. The checklist in our Tools section walks you through it.

    Do you give quotes before starting?

    Yes. We look at the problem, explain what's wrong, and give you the price. Work starts only after you say yes.

    How far do you travel?

    We cover everywhere within about 10 miles of Gladstone, MO — the Northland, North Kansas City, Liberty, Parkville, Riverside, downtown KC and nearby. Tap "Use my location" in the Service Area section to check your exact distance. Just outside the line? Call 816-879-9346 and ask.

    How do I keep pipes from freezing?

    Keep the heat at 55°F or higher, open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls, disconnect garden hoses, and let faucets drip on the coldest nights.
